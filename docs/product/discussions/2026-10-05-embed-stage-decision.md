## 1. Decision

The owner's rule, 2026-10-05, in his words: "all of that text probably shouldn't even be there. honestly maybe the only options should be pause play and settings for the visual changes that can be applied in real time."

Applied: while a reading is on screen in ChatGPT, the card shows the reading and two controls, **Play/Pause** and **Settings**. Settings holds only what changes the picture or the type at once: intensity (calmer to vivid), the nine colour themes, still imagery, text size. Begin is the first Play. Stop folds into Pause and the host's teardown. Every sentence that stood under the reading goes: the status line, the microphone note, the replay notice, the typed Visual change box, About this passage, the Transcript, the device notes. What a screen reader needs (the state, the words) lives in ARIA, never in visible prose. The one visible sentence left is a failure.

What it supersedes in `docs/product/CONSOLIDATED-READER.md` §5: the row "The embed's control set and the constructor guards | `controls.js`, `current.test.js`" (`:591`) and the C3 guard "Live and embed origins keep the `controls.js` set" (`:604`). For the embed only: the control set becomes the two objects above, in a new module; `controls.js` stays as it is for the standalone `/live` page; the constructor guards in `src/core/current.test.js:45-64` (one Session builder, one Player builder) are untouched. `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-01-chatgpt-demo.md:17` ("A persistent embedded notice explains this") is superseded: the poster carries the fact. `docs/product/tasks/LIVE-005.json:14` ("Stop cancels pending speech and future work") is met by Pause (the voice is held at once, `src/live/runtime.js:402-412`, `src/live/voices/browser.js:128-137`) and by teardown (everything cancelled, `src/live/host/LiveHost.js:592-599`); its wording changes with this record.

Fixed and untouched: `rise.current.v1` (schema, nine theme ids, three visual ids, 16 segments), `rise.experience-program.v1`, the compiler input and sentence chunking (`src/core/rise-current.js:293`), the Player API, `RISE_CURRENT_THEMES` (`rise-current.js:122-132`), `ATTRACTOR_VISUAL_MANIFEST` and `validateVisualCommand` (`src/core/visual-control-contract.js:1-43`), the admission path (`rise_present`'s `call()` at `worker/mcp-server.mjs:168-192`, `CURRENT_GUIDE`, `worker/mcp-gate0.mjs`).

Scope: everything the Composer edition is meant to do, polished. Out: Dive, Surface, the microphone, the realtime adapters, later model calls into an open widget (`docs/product/discussions/2026-10-04-composer-decision.md:15,24,29-30`).

## 2. The card

The card is one box, the stage: the theme's ground edge to edge, the Chamber's field and text panel, and two 44 px round glass objects, Play/Pause at the bottom-left and Settings at the bottom-right. The box has one height per width (§5) and never changes it on its own, so the thread does not jump at Play. Nothing inside scrolls: "No nested scrolling. Cards should auto fit their content and prevent internal scrolling" (https://developers.openai.com/plugins/concepts/ui-guidelines). Two controls: "Support up to two actions maximum, with one primary CTA and one optional secondary CTA" (same page).

Hidden elements, present in every state after admission: `p.rise-stage__status` (`role="status"`, `aria-live="polite"`, visually hidden) for the state sentence; `ol.rise-stage__text` (`aria-label="The whole reading"`, visually hidden) holding every sentence of the Current from admission, since the adapter pushes the whole Current at once (`src/live/adapters/mcp-app.js:127-133`); `p.rise-stage__said` (`aria-live="polite"`, visually hidden) that takes each committed sentence only while the reading is silent, and is emptied and set `aria-live="off"` while a voice speaks. The words are reachable without sight or sound (the Transcript's purpose, `src/live/host/controls.js:8-9`), and a silent screen-reader user hears whole sentences, not the word-by-word reveal that `aria-live` on `#atom-display` would read (`.atom-word[data-pending]`, `src/components/read/Chamber.js:2791-2792, 2884`). Judges 1 and 3 asked for the list; judges 1 and 2 asked for progress while silent; the committed-sentence region gives both without the chatter judge 3 rejected.

## 5. Sizing and the host

**Display modes.** `ui/initialize` sends `appCapabilities: { availableDisplayModes: ['inline'] }` (today `{}` at `src/live/hosts/mcp-port.js:203`): "View MUST declare all display modes it supports in `appCapabilities.availableDisplayModes` during initialization" (apps.mdx). The resource carries `_meta['openai/ui'].availableDisplayModes: ['inline']`: "Set `_meta[openai/ui].availableDisplayModes` on the resource contents returned by your server" and "Keep declaring `availableDisplayModes` during MCP Apps initialization too" (https://developers.openai.com/plugins/reference). Inline only, because "Host MUST NOT switch the View to a display mode that does not appear in its `appCapabilities.availableDisplayModes`, if set" (apps.mdx) binds the host, and because fullscreen's own rule cannot be met: "The composer is always present in fullscreen, so make sure your experience supports conversational prompts that can trigger tool calls" (ui-guidelines); "Design the experience to work with ChatGPT's composer, which remains available in fullscreen" (chatgpt-ui). A Composer widget takes no later call (composer-decision `:24`). The inline card's own example is "an audio player" (ui-guidelines), which is what this card is. All three judges rejected the Full screen row; it would also be a fifth row outside the owner's rule. Picture-in-picture is not declared either: OpenAI's own extension spec says ChatGPT supports inline and fullscreen only (apps-sdk-research map, confirmed), and its rules need composer reactions too. Adding fullscreen later is additive.

**Host context.** LiveHost reads `port.hostContext()` after `connect()` and subscribes with `port.onHostContext()` (both exist, `mcp-port.js:211-220`; the port merges field by field, "the View SHOULD merge received fields with its current context state", apps.mdx). What each field drives: `containerDimensions.height` fixed → the page fills it ("Host controls the size. View should fill the available space"); `containerDimensions.maxHeight` → the cap below; `width` and `maxWidth` → nothing, the card is always the frame's width; `safeAreaInsets` → `--safe-top/right/bottom/left` on the stage root, added to the objects' and the sheet's insets ("Safe area boundaries in pixels"; the repo's `env()` insets are 0 in frames, `design-system.css:173`); `styles.variables` → `--font-sans` and `--border-radius-md` for the sheet, each with a fallback ("Views should set default fallback values", apps.mdx; ChatGPT sends them since 2026-05-28, https://developers.openai.com/plugins/changelog); `theme` → stored, drives nothing visible (the stage keeps the reading's ground, Q6); `displayMode`, `locale`, `platform`, `deviceCapabilities` → stored; `hover` decides only whether the hover lightening applies.

**The one height.** `stageHeight = clamp(round(width × 0.66), 481, 560)`, then `min(stageHeight, containerDimensions.maxHeight)` when the host gives one. The 481 floor is a fact, not taste: the Chamber's phone-landscape block applies to any frame at most 900 px wide, at most 480 px tall and wider than tall (`Chamber.css:2250`) and would set the words at `clamp(17px, 3.6vh, 23px)` with no `--font-size-intent`, about 15–17 px, and the Text size row would do nothing. All three judges rejected the alternative `clamp(360, 0.75w, 560)` for this reason. Results: 390 → 481; 560 → 481; 760 → 502; 1280 → 560. The waiting card, the poster, the reading and the end share the box. Judge 1 noted that "Avoid fixed heights" still bites the waiting and poster states; accepted, because a card that grows at Play would move the conversation under the reader's finger, and the poster's empty ground is the theme's own.

**Reporting.** `ui/notifications/size-changed { height }` after the handshake and whenever the computed height changes: a `ResizeObserver` on the stage root plus every host-context change, coalesced to one animation frame, sent only on a changed value ("The View SHOULD send this notification when rendered content body size changes (e.g. using ResizeObserver API)"; for flexible dimensions hosts "MUST listen for `ui/notifications/size-changed` … and update the iframe dimensions accordingly", apps.mdx; "Avoid fixed heights", troubleshooting). Width is never reported: the port already drops a non-finite field (`mcp-port.js:255-260`, pinned by `mcp-port.test.js:490-493`), the host owns the width, and a once-reported width was the suspected, unverified cause of the right-edge clipping (stage-sizing map). This replaces the fixed 640 sent once (`LiveHost.js:36, 603`).

**Fullscreen.** Not requested and not declared. If a host switches the view anyway, the same layout fills `containerDimensions` and the objects keep clear of `safeAreaInsets.bottom`. The Chamber's browser Fullscreen button (`Chamber.js:1084-1099`), which cannot work in the sandbox, leaves with the chrome (§7), and the relay's `allow` drops `fullscreen` with it: `allow="autoplay"` (`mcp-relay.js:76`). `autoplay` stays, as the judges asked: speech synthesis and Web Audio start on the press inside RISE's frame, and the delegation is unverified either way.

## 12. Risks

- **Nothing was run.** Every pixel size is computed from the measured baseline and the CSS. PR 3's render at 760, 560 and 390 is the first proof, and the plan stops there if the numbers are wrong.
- **ChatGPT's real inline `containerDimensions`, `maxHeight` and width were never recorded.** A fixed height under 481 on a card wider than tall puts the words at 15–17 px through `Chamber.css:2250-2281`. The lasting fix is the `data-embed` exclusion in the Reader lane; the witness session decides whether it is needed now.
- **The Reader-lane change is shared code** (`Chamber.js`, the factory). The standalone `/live` page loses the Chamber bar with it; its suites are inferred not to use it, not run.
- **Genesis paused and resized.** #420 covers the attractor (`attractor.js:346-347`); `KleeField._resize` begins a fresh episode (`klee-field.js:129-133`), and whether a paused composition survives a rotation is not verified. A paused genesis card may go blank until Play.
- **Intensity re-sent at each passage.** The director cancels a control on every new cue (`visual-field-director.js:36-38`), so the reader sees a 320 ms ease from 0.65 to their value at each attractor passage. If that reads as a flicker, the director should keep a reader's control across cues: a Reader-lane change.
- **Colour on an attractor passage** changes the palette in place but keeps the filament's system and form until the next passage (`setPalette` only; `setForm` exists but is not called). Jade over Classic shows aizawa in jade until the next passage. Honest, and cheaper than a remount that would restart the motion.
- **Play again rebuilds the runtime.** `runtime.start` throws on a used runtime (`runtime.js:372`); the old runtime is stopped first, `embeddedBeginStarted` is reset deliberately, and the voice restarts on a fresh press. The end must never call `LiveHost.ended()`, which closes the port.
- **Chrome's user-activation rule for `speechSynthesis`**: Play and Play again are clicks inside RISE's frame, so activation exists; a host that synthesises clicks, as the LIVE-002 automation did, may not grant it. Unverified.
- **Very long sentences at 390 px**: sentence chunking (fixed) can produce a 300-character sentence, nine lines in a 481 px card, clipped by the field's `overflow: hidden` (`Chamber.css:101`). The models' guide should keep sentences under about 160 characters; that is Composer-lane prose in `current-guide.js`, outside this change.
- **Custom fonts.** The words and the title stay in Crimson Pro although the guideline says "Don't use custom fonts, even in full screen modes"; the plugin guidelines call these the optional UI guidelines, and the face is the content.
