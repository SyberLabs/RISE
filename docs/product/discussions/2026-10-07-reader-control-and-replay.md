## The five lines

| M2 asks | Where it stands | Held by |
|---|---|---|
| Begin, pause, resume and Stop are predictable; Stop cancels pending speech and future work | Begin is the poster's Play. Stop folds into Pause (the voice held at once) and the host's `ui/resource-teardown`, which stops the runtime: every timer, voice and connection released. Late exits and teardown during a slow start build nothing | `runtime.test.js` "stopping"; `LiveHost.test.js` "does not recreate playback after teardown during delayed runtime startup", the late exit-callback cases; `e2e/live-mcp.spec.js` "the host's ping is answered, and its request to tear down is answered and ends the reading" |
| Keep the useful local controls; say which are session preferences and which durable intent | Still imagery and Text size are the reader's, kept in the frame's own settings; Intensity and Theme are for this reading only (the Current's theme is the author's) | the embed stage decision, §Settings |
| Place is kept through pause, resume and navigation; late callbacks never restart stopped or replaced playback | A pause, a hidden page or a closed card silences the voice and remembers the phrase on screen; on Play the voice and the words take that phrase up together. Nothing pauses the browser's shared speech engine, so a card taken away while held leaves no silence behind ([#484](https://github.com/SyberLabs/RISE/pull/484)). A closed run ignores its voice's and Player's late reports | `dive-voice.test.js` "held and taken up again without a Dive"; `voices/browser.test.js` "the speech engine, which every page in the browser shares"; the runtime's `run.closed` guards |
| Controls work at phone widths, by keyboard, and with reduced motion | Both objects stay whole in view from 420 px wide in a 481 px frame; every Settings row is a native control; the poster's Play and Play again hand the focus to the object that replaces them, so a keyboard reader goes Enter, Space, Tab, Escape without losing their place; the system's reduced motion holds the imagery still mid-reading | `e2e/live-mcp.spec.js` "from the keyboard alone…", "in a short frame both objects stay whole in view…", "under reduced motion…", "a maximum valid CJK title keeps Play reachable in phone-sized host frames"; `stage-controls.test.js` focus cases |
| Save or export the admitted explanation through the interchange/Vault machinery, and verify replay | **Not in the embed** (decided here, reversible): the card shows two controls by the owner's direction, and a Vault inside ChatGPT's partitioned frame storage would be core state where the platform asks for none. Replay is the host's: a reload redelivers the result and shows the poster again with no autoplay, and Play again rebuilds the reading from the admitted answer | `e2e/live-mcp.spec.js` "reopening the nested frame reinitializes and plays the host result from its first passage"; `LiveHost.test.js` "on Play again, stops the finished runtime, builds a new one…" |
