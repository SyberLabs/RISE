Your system asks for reduced motion. In RISE the voice would move nothing for you; here the effects are on so they can be judged.

What drives what. A voice file and the microphone are measured as sound, every frame (the real thing, as an ElevenLabs voice would be). The device voice cannot be measured: browsers never hand its sound to the page, only the moment each word starts. Its signal is a stand-in built from those moments, each word swelling by its syllables; where a voice sends no word timings, the stand-in times the words itself. Stand-in, silent is that timed stand-in with no sound, for judging the motion alone. Try a file from voice-samples/ in the main folder against the device voice to see the gap.

Kept to. The voice moves shape, not light: what follows each syllable is position and outline, and brightness follows the voice by a fifth at most, in thin lines and soft glows, never a whole field. The face's eye stays seated; the pupil looks within the iris. The visual shown is the Thomas attractor, drawn more simply than RISE draws it; the warp is applied to its points on the screen, which is where RISE's own attractor would take it.
