## 3. Health and safety — please read this one

**Photosensitivity.** RISE renders moving light, and some compositions flash or
change brightness rapidly. A small number of people can experience seizures
when exposed to flashing light or patterns, including people with no history of
seizures or epilepsy.

The Service shows a photosensitivity warning before a session that may flash,
and offers a setting to disable the effects. **If you have epilepsy or any
photosensitive condition, use that setting, and consult a doctor before using
the Service.**

Stop immediately and seek medical advice if you experience dizziness, altered
vision, eye or face twitching, disorientation, involuntary movement, or loss of
awareness.

**Audio.** RISE plays soundscapes and spoken recitation. Set your volume to a
comfortable level before you begin, and take breaks.

**Attention.** Do not use the Service while driving, operating machinery, or
doing anything else that requires your attention.
